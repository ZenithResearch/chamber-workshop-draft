Adoption — opportunities and risks

Adoption requires more than just handing your employees a ChatGPT subscription and praying for the best.

Before you can automate work or dispatch and deploy agents to work in your business, you must lay out the map for them to follow.

  1. Map the work. Describe the business processes: their purpose, inputs, steps, decisions, responsibilities and expected outputs.
  2. Analyze them. Examine scalability, efficiency, bottlenecks and sources of delay or rework.
  3. Optimize them. What logical changes can you make to improve how the work is performed?
  4. Introduce new technology. Which tools will help you solve the problems you have identified?
  5. Automate. Automate suitable tasks with defined permissions, review criteria and points for human judgment.
