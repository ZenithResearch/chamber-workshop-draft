Access — defining who may do what

The problem: Giving someone a task doesn’t establish what they may disclose, change or commit the business to—or when they must ask.

  • Specify which people, software, agents and providers may read, change, request, share or approve.
  • Distinguish access to information from authority to act or make commitments.
  • Define permissions for processing, retention, reuse and training where relevant.
  • Connect assignment to both capability and authority, including when the work must return to a person for approval.
  • Distinguish permissions described in the model from controls enforced by software.
