Service requests — coordinate relationships and unfamiliar work

  • Define what one person, agent or organization can request from another.
  • Establish required information, expected outcomes, authority and commitments.
  • Support clarification, negotiation, acceptance, rejection and changes of scope.
  • Handle novelty by recognizing when an existing service definition is insufficient and routing the request for judgment or a revised agreement.
  • Coordinate routine communication between environments while preserving the points where people must decide.
  • Explore a structure for working out what the work should be, similar to how people clarify a request before committing to it.

Automation needs a way to ask, clarify and agree, as well as a way to execute.
