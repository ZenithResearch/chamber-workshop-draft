Sovereignty and competitive advantage

Share the foundations. Retain control over what makes your business distinctive.

Regaining sovereignty starts with data:

  • Define your business. Control how your information, relationships and operating knowledge are represented.
  • Choose what to share and delegate. Decide which capabilities to develop yourself, obtain from others or build together.
  • Develop your advantage. Use your knowledge to train models, discover insights and create new capabilities.
  • Choose where value flows. Decide who can access, use and benefit from your data—including opportunities to monetize it.
