Decision making — choosing how work is performed

The problem: Routine choices consume attention; similar requests receive inconsistent treatment; exceptions depend on someone remembering the rules.

  • Open the decision black box: identify its inputs, available choices, decision and assessment criteria.
  • Introduce algorithmic methods, LLMs and System One models alongside human judgment and approval. Define “System One models” before using the term in the presentation.
  • Show how reviewed examples, choices and corrections can help systems learn to approximate how we make decisions.
  • Capture the context and reasons behind corrections. Feedback requires a mechanism for updating rules, instructions, examples or models.
  • Introduce graph algorithms for suitable optimization problems, including routing, matching and dependency ordering.
  • Combine approaches deliberately: interpret a request, calculate options and approve exceptions.
  • Distinguish learning to imitate existing decisions from improving those decisions.
