Operational efficiency

Mapping and tracking the work makes it possible to improve the process and automate decisions we can verify.

  • Map: steps, decisions, handoffs and what success means.
  • Track: what happened, where work waited and what needed rework.
  • Evaluate and evolve: test changes against measurable outcomes.
  • Automate: decisions and routing where the rules are clear and success can be checked.

Two ways to improve

  • Lower the cost: run the same process with fewer resources, while maintaining quality.
  • Improve how it scales: redesign the process so the work required grows more slowly as volume increases.
