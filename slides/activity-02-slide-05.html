Evolution — retaining improvements

The problem: Staff correct the same mistakes repeatedly, while useful corrections fail to become retained improvements to processes, tools or models.

  • Capture feedback, corrections and failures as candidates for improvement within each person’s environment.
  • Introduce evolutionary harnesses: observe → propose a change → evaluate → approve or reject → retain the approved version.
  • Apply changes to SOPs, rules, instructions, implementations, models or assignments.
  • Identify who may propose, evaluate and approve changes, including changes affecting shared interfaces, responsibilities or authority.
  • Preserve version history and the ability to return to a previous approach.
  • Connect this cycle to making effective use of Hermes within the business’s operating rules.
