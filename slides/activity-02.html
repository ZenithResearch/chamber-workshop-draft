Data foundations — making work understandable across boundaries

The problem: People repeatedly reconcile applications, find missing information and explain context before work can begin.

  • Each person needs an evolving working environment suited to their responsibilities, with well-defined interfaces, responsibilities and access.
  • Introduce the core ontology as shared structures and vocabulary for describing the business, its relationships and the interfaces between working environments.
  • Begin with inputs → function doing the work → outputs.
  • Describe the function’s purpose, required information, operating logic and promised result. Make responsibility and authority explicit.
  • Expand the model to expose dependencies, handoffs, decisions and exceptions.
  • Let people retain and develop knowledge within their own environments while making the information required for an interaction explicit.
