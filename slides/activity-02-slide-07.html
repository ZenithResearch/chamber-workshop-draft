ZENITHFoundations

A business that can learn from its work

Connect the foundations through a cycle: describe → decide and act → record → evaluate → improve → repeat.

  • Data foundations: describe the business, its tasks and relationships.
  • Decision making: choose how work is performed.
  • Provenance: preserve what happened and the context behind it.
  • Evaluation: assess models and environments to guide training and improvement.
  • Evolution: test changes and retain approved improvements.
  • Access: define how each working environment interacts with others and with shared resources, including who may observe, act, evaluate, change and approve. Each retains its own space, evolving around its responsibilities.

Takeaway: Each completed task can contribute evidence for improving how the business operates—while the business retains control over its knowledge, decisions and changes.

Transition: Participants will build a small instance of this structure in the practical work.
