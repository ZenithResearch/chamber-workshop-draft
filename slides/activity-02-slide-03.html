Provenance — knowing what happened and why

The problem: When a result is challenged—or needed for improvement—the business cannot easily reconstruct which information, rule, model or approval produced it.

  • Identify authoritative information and its sources.
  • Connect a request to the information, rules, decisions, actions and results it produced.
  • Record who or what performed the work, which versions were used and where approval occurred.
  • Preserve corrections and exceptions so results can be inspected, investigated and used in improvement.
