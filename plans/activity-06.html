ZENITH

Questions

Summary

Address questions arising from the workshop and help participants clarify their business decision and next step.

Business relevance

Give members space to examine adoption effort, data control, efficiency, software dependencies and the implications for their own responsibilities.

Intended outcome

Participants understand what has been answered, what needs further investigation and how that affects their next experiment.

Bulleted plan

  • Invite questions about the concepts, demonstration, exercise and business mapping.
  • Revisit relevant examples and clarify terminology.
  • Discuss implications through self-sovereignty, efficiency and verifiability.
  • Record unresolved questions and the investigation or follow-up they require.
  • Close by inviting participants to state their decision and practical next step.

Evidence of success

Participants can articulate a reasoned decision and next step. Unanswered questions are recorded with their follow-up requirements.
