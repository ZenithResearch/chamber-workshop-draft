ZENITH

Exercise

Agenda

Summary

Guide participants through getting started with the working environment, exploring how work is organized, connecting Anima, making service requests and examining formal verification. Exercise scenario and delivery readiness: To determine.

Business relevance

Give members practical experience with an approach to AI adoption that connects their information and responsibilities to useful work, while retaining control over their environment and software dependencies.

Intended outcome

Participants gain hands-on experience, identify what the approach would require in their business, make a useful adoption decision and define a practical next experiment.

Bulleted plan

  1. Install from Lightweight Hub
    • Guide participants through the intended installation and initial setup.
    • Identify what environment they are creating and what it will need access to.
    • Installation readiness, prerequisites and available assistance: To determine.
  2. DevGraph
    • Introduce how the exercise’s project and work are represented.
    • Guide participants through the selected actions in DevGraph.
    • Participant actions and relationship to the Demo project: To determine.
  3. Connect to Anima
    • Guide participants through the intended connection.
    • Explain the information, responsibilities and permissions involved.
    • Connection method and supported exercise flow: To determine.
  4. Service requests
    • Define a request’s intended result, required information and review criteria.
    • Guide participants through the selected request and review process.
    • Service, participant actions and expected output: To determine.
  5. Formal verification of work
    • Identify the work or property to be checked and how the result should be interpreted.
    • Distinguish that check from the wider assessment of whether the work meets the business’s needs.
    • Verification scope, method, tooling and hands-on component: To determine.

Evidence of success

Participants can describe what they personally attempted, inspect the resulting work or evidence, identify a requirement or limitation relevant to their business, and state a reasoned decision and next experiment. How this is recorded: To determine.
