Slides

Summary

Set up the working environment, create an SOP and use it to frame service requests and examine verification. Scenario and delivery readiness: To determine.

Business relevance

Explore manageable AI adoption, useful business information and efficient work while retaining control over tools and dependencies.

Intended outcome

Gain hands-on experience, draft an SOP, make a useful business decision and identify a practical next experiment.

Bulleted plan

  1. Install from Lightweight Hub — Begin setup; prerequisites and installation readiness are to determine.
  2. DevGraph — Explore prioritization and planning, then algorithmic project selection based on KPIs.
  3. Connect to Anima — Introduce the connection and its information and permission boundaries.
  4. Create an SOP — Describe a recurring task using the proposed SOP materials.
  5. Service requests — Use the SOP to frame a request and review its intended result.
  6. Formal verification of work — Examine an agreed check; its scope, method and tooling are to determine.

Evidence of success

Participants can describe what they attempted, review their SOP and any resulting work, and state a reasoned decision and next experiment. How this is recorded: To determine.

To determine.
